## 1. codetables.de semantics for q = 4 (task 1 — NOT settled)

* `codetables.de/QECC.php?q=2…` is titled "Bounds on the minimum distance of **additive** quantum codes" (qubit codes, self-dual
  codes over GF(2²); upper bounds from a MAGMA program of Rains). The same URL with q = 3 returns "currently not available for q=3";
  with q = 4 it silently returns the qubit page. The q > 2 tables sit behind `QECC/Tables_color.php` (dynamic) and no WebFetch
  returned the q = 4 notes or the [[8,0]]_4 entry itself.
* errorcorrectionzoo.org (`qudit_stabilizer`): "Distance upper bounds for Galois-qudit stabilizer codes for various n and k …
  maintained by M. Grassl … hold for general modular-qudit codes because they are based on linear programming." So the q > 2
  *upper* bounds are advertised as LP bounds valid for all stabilizer codes (not just F_q-linear ones).
* `data/sources/codetables_k0_2026-10-10.json` meta (written by the swarm setup, not by Grassl): "F_q-linear stabilizer codes".
* Checked in the local Huber–Wyderka snapshot (`work/auditor/table_cells.py`, which parses the Bokeh column arrays):
  there is NO row for AME(8,4) at all (blank = unknown), and the comment "Cannot be a stabilizer state." (ref Danielsen 1106.2428,
  Hermitian self-dual additive codes over GF(9)) occurs on exactly ONE cell, AME(11,3). So the table authors do not record any
  stabilizer exclusion for AME(8,4); the exclusion of F_4-linear codes in this swarm's brief rests on codetables.de alone.
  Other table facts: AME(8,3) "No (Shadow)"; AME(8,6) Unknown (ref Goyeneche–Życzkowski 1404.3586); AME(11,8) and AME(13,8)
  are "propagation from AME(12,8)" / "AME(14,8)" (so AME(12,8), AME(14,8) are in the table as existing).
* Inference (NOT a fact from a source): the AME table lists AME(8,4) as open although it applies the shadow/LP bounds valid for
  all states; hence the [[8,0]]_4 ≤ 4 in codetables is probably not a bound that every F_2-linear/Z_4 stabilizer state must obey
  (or the AME table would say "nonexistent"). Equivalently: the weight-enumerator of an AME(8,4) stabilizer state is the MDS one
  (A_5..A_8 = 840, 4620, 21000, 39075 over the 16-letter alphabet, all divisible by 15, nonnegative integers), so integrality /
  divisibility alone does not kill it. I could not verify whether the codetables bound uses F_4-linearity; the safe reading is
  "F_4-linear excluded; F_2-linear and Z_4 stabilizers are not covered by any statement I could find".
  My own shadow test (work/auditor/shadow_check.py, qubit-style sign) is NOT valid for d > 2 (it flags the existing AME(4,3),
  AME(8,5), AME(10,4) as violated) and should not be used.

## 7. Phase 2: does the literature already decide the F_2-linear stabilizer AME(8,4)? (auditor, 23:00; search summaries only)
* Ball-Gamboa-Lavrauw, "On additive MDS codes over small fields", arXiv:2012.06183 (Adv. Math. Commun. 2021): classifies additive MDS codes over alphabets F_4, F_8, F_9 (verifies the additive MDS conjecture there). Our object is an F_2-additive (8, 2^16, 5) MDS code over the alphabet F_2^4 of size 16 (not 4, 8 or 9), so that paper does NOT decide it. Abstract read (WebFetch 23:02): setting is additive MDS codes over F_{q^2} that are F_q-linear; main theorem n >= q + k with k+1 F_{q^2}-linear projections => linear; classifies ALL additive MDS codes over F_4, F_8, F_9 and only "the longest additive MDS codes over F_16 which are linear over F_4" (not F_2-linear ones, not shorter lengths). Our F_2-linear alphabet-16 length-8 code is therefore outside their classification (consistent with the swarm's earlier F_4-linear exclusion being the case they cover). Full text not read.
* Griesmer-type bounds for additive codes, arXiv:2406.08916 Sec. 10.1: a table of additive MDS codes over F_4 with an asterisked length-8 entry (r = 5), unclear relation to ours (alphabet 4, not 16).
* Wojcik et al. 2603.18193 (graph states in even d) and Cha 2603.13442: do not decide F_2-linear AME(8,4); 2608.01011 still lists AME(8,4) as the first open case (web search 22:58). So the exhaustive result of the swarm (argument.md 5.13: no F_2-linear stabilizer AME(8,4)) is not contradicted by anything found and appears to be new, but a dedicated novelty check on additive MDS codes over alphabet 16 (Ball-Gamboa-Lavrauw follow-ups, Bierbrauer et al. on additive quantum codes) is still to be done before it is written up.
