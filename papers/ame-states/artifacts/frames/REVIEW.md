### 5.12 V-picture of stabilizer AME(2h,4) and an independent n = 8 search (auditor, phase 2, 2026-10-16 23:00; review of classify #315)
**Reduction (re-derived).** S = {(u, Mu)} with u ∈ A = parties 0..h−1, M: A → B = W_h ⊕ … ⊕ W_{2h−1} symplectic; A_j = party j of A, V_j := M(A_j) (h mutually orthogonal nondegenerate 4-dim subspaces, B = ⊕V_j). An element of S supported on J ⊂ A-parties and L ⊂ B-parties exists iff V_J ∩ W_L ≠ 0 (for |J|+|L| = h this means V_J + W_L ≠ B; symplectic duality V_J^⊥ = V_{J^c}, W_L^⊥ = W_{L^c} identifies (J,L) with (J^c,L^c)). Isometries A_j → V_j form an Sp(4,2)-torsor, so S up to A-side local Cliffords is the tuple (V_j); number of labelled S = 720^h × #ordered valid tuples. Equivalent weight form (no M needed, `vbrute8.py`): min party weight of S = min over J ≠ ∅ and nonzero m_j ∈ V_j (j ∈ J) of |J| + wt(Σ m_j).
**Controls.** (a) n = 4: 12 of 28 forms Q admissible, 8640 ordered pairs, N(4) = 720²·8640 = 4478976000 = |G|/1440 (my independent orbit count `ame44_classes.py`). (b) n = 6: N(6) = 720⁵·54400 (`vcount.py`); mass N/(720⁶·6!) = 17/162.
**n = 8, V_0 classes (`vclasses8.py`).** V_0 = {(x, φ_5 x, φ_6 x, φ_7 x)} (every V_j is a graph over every party), only (Q_5, Q_6, Q_7) = pullbacks of J matter; 9600 of 28³ triples admissible; 72 orbits under Sp(4,2) alone, 10 under Sp(4,2)⁴ × S_4 (orbit sizes 48, 72, 360, 480, 720, 720, 720, 1440, 2160, 2880).
**n = 8 search (`vsearch8.c`, own code).** For each V_0: V_1 = 4-subspace of V_0^⊥ inside the set {x : all 4 nibbles nonzero, wt(x + v_0) ≥ 3 ∀ v_0 ∈ V_0}, nondegenerate; then V_2 likewise inside (V_0+V_1)^⊥ with both V_0, V_1 pair-conditions; V_3 = remaining 4-dim space, checked directly (weights and pairs); subspaces enumerated with lexicographically least bases (`enumtest.c`: exactly 651, 11811, 200787 subspaces of F_2^6, F_2^7, F_2^8, and exact agreement with brute force on random candidate sets). Results on the 10 full-gauge reps (3.2–3.8 s each): n_V1 ∈ [24128, 28960], n_V2 ∈ [176, 8912], **n_SOL = 0 for all 10**. Relaxed control (single weight ≥ 3, pair weight ≥ 2, i.e. min party weight 4): millions of solutions; 50 of them brute-force verified with min party weight exactly 4 (`vbrute8.py`).
**Status.** Preliminary: NOT yet compared with classify's counts; the claim "no F_2-linear stabilizer AME(8,4)" requires agreement of two independent implementations and review of the shared reduction (above). Logs `work/auditor/run_v8_*.log`.

### 5.14 Why no cheap obstruction for AME(8,4) F_2-linear: quadratic-form refined LP, and the structure of the near-misses (auditor, phase 2, 2026-10-16 23:15; `work/auditor/qlp.py`, `qlp2.py`, `qlp3.py`, `qj5.py`, `near_supp8.py`, `dimJ_parity.py`)
Purpose: look for a THIRD, method-independent argument for the closure of 5.12/5.13 (the two exhaustive searches share the V-picture). Result: none found; the checks below are recorded so nobody repeats them.
(a) *Quadratic-form refinement.* For a Lagrangian S of ⊕_i V_i (V_i = F_2^4 symplectic) and ANY quadratic form Q with polarisation the symplectic form, Q|_S is additive (Q(u+v) = Q(u)+Q(v)+<u,v>), and every functional on S is <a,·>|_S (S^⊥ = S), so there is a with Q_a = Q + <a,·> vanishing on S. Q_a = ⊕ q_{a_i}, each local q hyperbolic (H: 10 zeros incl. 0) or elliptic (E: 6 zeros); S is totally singular for it. Complete enumerator over the classes {0, singular nonzero, nonsingular} per party, symmetrised within the H group and the E group, with the MacWilliams identity for S = S^⊥ (kernel matrices class-invariant, checked), N ≥ 0, N(0) = 1, Σ N = 2^16, words of weight 1..4 forbidden, even number of nonsingular parties per word. `qlp.py 4 3`, `6 4`, `8 5`: LP FEASIBLE for every even e (number of elliptic parties); negative control `qlp2.py 8 6` (min weight 6) INFEASIBLE for all e. For AME(8,4) the polytope is a point for e = 8 (all parties elliptic; integral, e.g. 560 weight-5 words with 4 nonsingular parties, 280 with 2) and has 12 / 56 / 78 / 58 non-fixed variables (an upper bound for its dimension, not the dimension itself) for e = 0 / 2 / 4 / 6. With weights 1..3 forbidden and A_4 prescribed, `qlp3.py 14`: every A_4 in 0..14 is LP-feasible for every e. So the LP (Shor–Laflamme/Rains-type, with the quadratic refinement) carries no obstruction.
(b) *Weight-5 sub-codes.* In an AME(8,4), S_J (|J| = 5) is 4-dimensional and its projection onto every party of J is an isomorphism, so five nondegenerate quadratic forms Q_i = q_i∘π_i on F_2^4 sum to 0. `qj5.py` (Walsh–Hadamard on the 1024-dimensional space of quadratic forms, 448 nondegenerate: 280 H, 168 E): ordered 5-tuples with sum 0 number 1,688,064,000 / 999,936,000 / 612,864,000 / 354,816,000 / 225,792,000 / 122,572,800 for 0..5 elliptic members. All compositions are realisable, no obstruction.
(c) *Near-misses (the 30 sampled for V_0 rep 0, `v8c_solutions.txt`).* The weight-4 words always sit on supports of shape (|J_A|,|J_B|) = (1,3) or (3,1) relative to the frame split, in COMPLEMENTARY PAIRS of supports (J, J^c) with equal multiplicity 3 (dim S_J = 2) or 15 (dim S_J = 4): (n supports, multiplicity): 18 near-misses with 2 supports x 15 (A_4 = 30), 6 with 2 x 3 (A_4 = 6), 2 with 4 x 3 (A_4 = 12), 4 with 6 x 3 (A_4 = 18). dim S_J = dim S_{J^c} is a general theorem (π_{J^c}(S)^⊥ = S_{J^c} and dim π_{J^c}(S) = 16 − dim S_J, S = S^⊥; verified by `dimJ_parity.py` on all (sample, split) pairs: 0 mismatches). **CORRECTION (23:20, supersedes the first draft of this paragraph and the evenness claim in board #338):** the 30 near-misses above are only the FIRST 30 found (run_v8n.py cap), a biased sample. Over ALL 24,234 (V_0 rep, V_1, V_2) candidates of the 10 full-gauge classes (`vsearch8h.c` = vsearch8.c plus a histogram, `run_v8h.py`, log `run_v8h.log`), where V_0..V_2 pass every condition and V_3 = (V_0+V_1+V_2)^perp fails only its single-element weights, the number c3 of nonzero V_3 words of party weight <= 3 (A_4 = 2 c3 by the J/J^c theorem, since (2,2)-supports are excluded by the pair conditions) is {3: 1912, 5: 36, 6: 1476, 8: 30, 9: 1028, 15: 19752}, never 0, 1, 2, 4, 7. In terms of k_b = dim{v in V_3 : nibble b of v = 0} (b = the four B-parties, sorted descending): (2,0,0,0): 1912; (2,1,1,0): 36; (2,2,0,0): 1476; (2,2,1,1): 30; (2,2,2,0): 1028; (4,0,0,0): 19752 (V_3 inside the sum of three B-parties). So single-word defects (k_b = 1) DO occur but only next to a k_b >= 2, and in EVERY near-miss some B-party has k_b >= 2, i.e. U = V_0+V_1+V_2 meets some B_b in dimension >= 2 (equivalently the condition V_J cap B_L = 0 for (J,L) = ({0,1,2},{b}) fails in dimension >= 2; counting predicts only ~0.94 expected nonzero elements per b). The minimum is A_4 = 6 (one bad 4|4 cut with a 2-dimensional S_J). This is the exact statement of 'V_3 never passes' in subspace terms; a conceptual reason is not found. The generic (relaxed) Lagrangians with min party weight >= 4 (23 of 300 graph-form samples, A_4 between 34 and 58) have dim S_J = 1 in 438 of 805 (sample, split) pairs, so dimension 1 is generic there. Not needed for the closure (exhaustive search); it would be the natural place for a conceptual proof.
Block-matrix reading of the same data (S = {(u, Mu)}, M = (M_{bj}) a 4x4 array of 4x4 F_2-blocks in Sp(16,2), V_j = M(A_j)): k_b = dim ker M_{b3} = 4 - rank M_{b3}. So the pattern (4,0,0,0), which is 19752 of the 24234 near-misses (81%), says that a whole block M_{b3} of the last column is the ZERO matrix, and every near-miss has a block of rank <= 2 in the last column (a random 4x4 F_2 block has rank <= 2 with probability only 0.11, rank 0 with 2e-5). By the Jacobi complementary-minor identity for symplectic M, 'M_{b3} singular' is the same as 'the 12x12 minor (rows other than b, columns 0,1,2) singular', i.e. these are the 3x3 block minors that the V_0..V_2 stages do not see; AME needs all 16 blocks, all 36 2x2 block minors (18 independent) and the 16 3x3 minors (16 dual to the blocks) nonsingular: 34 independent determinant conditions.

**PRIOR LITERATURE (auditor, 2026-10-16 23:45; supersedes the "appears to be new" wording of literature.md sec 7):** the same
nonexistence is already published. S. Ball, E. Moreno, R. Simoens, "Stabiliser codes over fields of even order", arXiv:2401.06618

NOT F_4-linear. Method: computer-assisted geometry (7 solids of PG(11,2) with symplectic polarities; classify all [[6,2,3]]_4
