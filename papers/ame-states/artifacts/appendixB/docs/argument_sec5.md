## 5. H-type stabilizer AME(8,4): reduction and independent exhaustion (auditor, shift 3; `work/auditor/hdfs.c`)

**Reduction.** One ququart party = 2 qubits, local symplectic group Sp(4,2) ≅ S6 (|Sp| = 720, `sp42_classes.py`). It has 80 elements
of order 3: 40 fixed-point-free (fpf; g²+g+1 = 0, one conjugacy class, centralizer of order 18 = |GU(2,2)|) and 40 with a fixed 2-space.
Assume the stabilizer S ⊂ F_2^32 (dim 16) is invariant under a product ρ = ⊕g_j of fpf order-3 local symmetries. Conjugating by local
Cliffords, all g_j are equal, so every party is F_4² (ω acts as g) and S is F_4-linear, dim_F4 S = 8 in F_4^16. The F_2-symplectic form
is Tr of an F_4-Hermitian form h (nondegenerate; standard h on an orthonormal basis); S isotropic and F_4-closed ⇒ h(x,y) = 0 on S
(Tr(h) = Tr(ωh) = 0), so S is Hermitian self-dual. AME ⇔ every 4 parties (8 columns) have full F_4-rank 8 (the F_2 rank criterion of §1
restricted to F_4-linear S). Parties 0..3 may be taken as an information set (AME makes every 4-set one): S = rowspace [I_8 | M],
M M† = I (conjugation x ↦ x²), party j = coordinates 2j, 2j+1 of the 16 columns. AME ⇔ every party minor M[R,C], |R| = |C| = k, with 2k×2k
entries, is nonsingular (k = 1..4). Local equivalences = GU(2,2)^8 (order 18 per party): M ↦ L M U with L, U block diagonal unitary.
**Normal form.** (a) U fixes the blocks X_c of row party 0 up to H_c = X_c X_c† (10 invertible 2×2 Hermitian matrices over F_2 / F_4, one
representative X_H each); orthonormality of row party 0 is Σ_c H_c = I. (b) L on row parties 1..3 fixes the frame: the 6 norm-1 vectors of a
nondegenerate Hermitian plane are the F_4^*-multiples of an orthonormal basis (r0, r1), so (r0, r1) is canonical when r0 and r1 are each minimal
in their multiple class and r0 < r1. Hence #solutions M = N_norm · 18^(2h−1), with h = n/2 row parties (18^h from U, 18^(h−1) from L on parties 1..h−1).
**Search.** Depth-first over the 624 starts (tuples (H_c)) then canonical frames of parties 1, 2, 3, pruning with the party minors (R ⊆ {0..p}
containing p). Code shares nothing with `work/pairs/dfs4.c`.
**Calibration (all passed).** h = 2 (n = 4): N_norm = 6 = 34992 / 18³, where 34992 is the independent full enumeration of the 77760 unitary
4×4 M (`hform.py`). h = 3 (n = 6): N_norm = 2074; random-unitary sampling (400000 samples, `hdfs h sample`) estimates 2062.9 ± 15.0;
prefix sampling P = 0: 64.3 ± 0.2 (DFS: 64 starts), P = 1: 2082.7 ± 15.1 (DFS 2074). h = 4 (n = 8): starts 624 (sampling 626.0 ± 2.0),
party-1 survivors 778584 (sampling 782851 ± 7708). Final check of every leaf uses a direct elimination of all C(8,4) = 70 column sets of G = [I | M].
**Result (n = 8).** 48 s: N_norm = 0 — party 2 candidates 186860160, passed 0 (so no H-type stabilizer AME(8,4)). With only the k ≤ 2 minors at
party 2: 322608 normalised near-solutions, completing the unique last frame, the failing 4-party sets (of 70, complementary pairs) number
2 (273072 nodes), 4 (28800), 6 (20736): the best H-type stabilizer state has exactly one bad cut of 35, never 0. This agrees with the
exhaustive negative H-type result of `work/pairs/dfs4*.c` (posts of @pairs, m = 4..7) obtained with a different normal form.

### 5.1 Odd-order local symmetries of a stabilizer AME(8,4) (own proof; closes the "≥ 5 non-fpf parties" loophole in @pairs' lemma)
Let ρ = ⊕ g_j be a local symplectic symmetry of the stabilizer S ⊂ V = F_2^32 (party j = 2 qubits, V_j = F_2^4), g_j ∈ Sp(4,2), ρ of order r ∈ {3, 5}
(|Sp(4,2)| = 720 = 2^4·3^2·5, so these are the only odd primes). Since r is odd, V = V_fix ⊕ V_ω (V_fix = ker(ρ−1) = ⊕ Fix_j, V_ω = ker(Φ_r(ρ))),
orthogonal for the symplectic form and both nondegenerate; S = (S∩V_fix) ⊕ (S∩V_ω). S∩V_fix is isotropic in V_fix (dim f) so dim ≤ f/2; S∩V_ω is
isotropic F_{2^m}-subspace (m = 2 for r = 3, m = 4 for r = 5) of V_ω (dim 32−f), so F_2-dim ≤ (32−f)/2. dim S = 16 forces equality in both: S = S_fix ⊕ S_ω with
S_fix Lagrangian in V_fix. Fix_j is nondegenerate (dim 0, 2 or 4: order 3 non-fpf has a 1-qubit Fix_j, identity has 2 qubits, order 5 has 0 or 4), so
party j's Hilbert space is (Fix_j qubits) ⊗ (rest) and the state is a PRODUCT φ ⊗ χ, φ the stabilizer state of S_fix on the parties T = {j : Fix_j ≠ 0}.
AME: for each 4-set A of parties ρ_A = φ_{A∩T} ⊗ χ_A is maximally mixed iff both factors are. (i) |T| ≤ 4: take A ⊇ T, φ_T is pure ⇒ not maximally mixed
unless T = ∅. (ii) 5 ≤ |T| ≤ 7: φ pure on T gives rank φ_A ≤ d_{T∖A} (product of local dims); choose A ⊂ T as 4 parties of largest local dimension, so every party of A
has dimension ≥ x := max local dim in T∖A (x ≥ 2), hence d_A ≥ x^4 > x^(|T|−4) ≥ d_{T∖A} as |T|−4 ≤ 3: φ_A cannot be maximally mixed. (iii) |T| = 8: d_A ≤ d_{T∖A} for the 4 largest forces all dims equal: all Fix_j = V_j (ρ = 1) or all Fix_j are 1 qubit
(r = 3) and φ is an 8-qubit pure state with every 4-subset maximally mixed = AME(8,2), which does not exist (Scott / Rains). Hence a nontrivial ρ of odd order has
T = ∅: ρ acts fixed-point-freely on EVERY party (r = 3: H-type, F_4-linear Hermitian; r = 5: F_16-linear, hence F_4-linear, i.e. S-type). Both are excluded by exhaustive
searches (H-type: @pairs and the auditor's hdfs.c; F_16/S-type: @pairs' DFS, the auditor's hsdfs.c and codetables). So any stabilizer AME(8,4) with F_2^2-per-party structure has a 2-group of local symmetries.

### 5.2 F_4-linear stabilizers with non-symplectic F_4-structures (auditor, shift 3; `work/auditor/f4struct.py`, `hsdfs.c`)
S is F_4-linear w.r.t. Ω = ⊕ω_j (ω_j ∈ GL(4,2), ω_j² + ω_j + 1 = 0, NOT necessarily symplectic). There are 112 such ω; Sp(4,2) has exactly three orbits, by the
B-adjoint ω* = J^{-1}ω^T J: **H** (ω* = ω^{-1}, 40 elements, ω ∈ Sp), **S** (ω* = ω, 12), **M** (ω*, ω do not commute, 60; ⟨ω, ω*⟩ is a 4-dim non-commutative local
algebra A, |A| = 16, rad A = N with |N| = 4, A/N = F_4, F_2^4 ≅ A free of rank 1, unique proper submodule N·V of size 4). Local Cliffords make ω_j the representative of its orbit.
Write B = Tr(β + σ), β F_4-bilinear, σ F_4-sesquilinear (every F_2-bilinear form on F_4^2 is uniquely so). H ⇒ β = 0, S ⇒ σ = 0 (apply x ↦ ωx, y ↦ ωy to the
invariance conditions). S = S^⊥ and F_4-closed: Tr(β+σ) = Tr(ω(β+σ)) = 0 on S×S gives β+σ = 0, and replacing y by Ωy gives ωβ + ω̄σ = 0; with ω + ω̄ = 1 this is β = σ = 0 on S×S.
**Mixed H+S is impossible**: π_S(S) is β-isotropic in the nondegenerate S-part (F_4-dim ≤ a_S), π_H(S) is σ-isotropic (≤ a_H), a_S + a_H = 8 = dim_F4 S, so S = π_S(S) ⊕ π_H(S): a
product state across (H | S) parties; the smaller group has ≤ 4 parties and a pure reduced state. So uniform H (two exhaustive codes) and uniform S (two exhaustive codes) are the
only H/S cases. **Uniform M is impossible**: A is a finite chain ring (non-commutative, N² = 0), so S ≅ A^{k1} ⊕ (A/N)^{k2}, 2k1 + k2 = 8, T := S ∩ N V^8 is an F_4-linear code of
dimension k1 + k2 = 8 − k1 inside N^8 ≅ F_4^8 with minimum party weight ≥ 5 (AME), so 8 − k1 ≤ 4 (Singleton) hence k1 = 4, k2 = 0, and T is an [8,4,5]_4 MDS code, which does
not exist (a nontrivial MDS code over F_4 has n ≤ 6).
**M parties mixed with H/S parties (a_M ≥ 1, a_O := a_H + a_S ≥ 1) are impossible too.** Let R = F_2[Ω, Ω*] ⊂ End(V); it acts on the M parties as A and on the
other parties through a quotient of R that is a field (F_4, as ω_j* = ω_j^{±1}), so a nilpotent element of R acts as 0 there. R ⊂ A × F_4 is subdirect. Either R = A × F_4, whose
central idempotent splits S into a product across (M | others), impossible as above (the smaller group has ≤ 4 parties and a pure reduced state); or R ≅ A (a ↦ (a, φ(a)), φ: A → A/N = F_4)
and then N acts as 0 on the non-M parties. S is a module over the chain ring A, S ≅ A^{k1} ⊕ (A/N)^{k2}, 2k1 + k2 = 8. (i) T := N·S ≅ N^{k1} is an F_4-linear code of dimension k1
supported on the a_M M-parties (alphabet N V_j ≅ F_4), minimum party weight ≥ 5, so T ≠ 0 ⇒ a_M ≥ 5 and k1 ≤ a_M − 4. (ii) S_0 := {s : N s = 0} has |S_0| = 4^(8−k1), lives in
alphabets of size 4 on M parties and 16 on the others, and has minimum party weight ≥ 5, so its projection onto any 4 parties is injective: |S_0| ≤ ∏ of the four smallest alphabets.
If a_M ≥ 4: 4^(8−k1) ≤ 4^4 ⇒ k1 = 4, then (i) needs a_M ≥ 8 (a_O = 0). If 1 ≤ a_M ≤ 3: 4^(8−k1) ≤ 4^(a_M)·16^(4−a_M) ⇒ k1 ≥ a_M ≥ 1 ⇒ T ≠ 0 with support on ≤ 3 parties,
contradiction. Hence a_M = 0, and the H/S-only cases are closed by the exhaustive codes (uniform H, uniform S) and the product argument (mixed). **Conclusion: there is no stabilizer
AME(8,4) (party = 2 qubits) that is F_4-linear with respect to a fixed-point-free F_4-structure on every party.** Not covered: F_4-structures with fixed points, F_2-linear states
without such a structure, Z_4-type groups beyond lemma #31, non-stabilizer states.

### 5.3 Odd-order local LINEAR automorphisms (no symplecticity, fixed points allowed) of a stabilizer AME(8,4) (auditor, shift 4; `work/auditor/fixpt_count.py`)
**Claim.** Let S ⊂ V = ⊕_{j=1}^8 F_2^4 be a stabilizer AME(8,4) (dim 16, every nonzero element of party weight ≥ 5) and Ω = ⊕ ω_j with ω_j ∈ GL(4,2), Ω S = S, Ω ≠ 1 of odd
prime order ℓ ∈ {3, 5, 7} (ω_j ∈ GL(4,2) ≅ A_8 has order dividing ℓ; no assumption that ω_j preserves the symplectic form). Then S is F_4-linear for a fixed-point-free F_4-structure
on every party (ℓ = 3 or 5), and that case is excluded by 5.2; all other fixed-point patterns and ℓ = 7 are excluded by counting.
*Proof.* (0) AME ⇒ the projection of S onto any ≤ 4 parties is onto (S = S^⊥, S has no element supported on ≤ 4 parties). If Ω|_S = 1 then ω_j = 1 on the image V_j of S, so Ω = 1; hence Ω|_S ≠ 1.
(1) ℓ odd ⇒ S = S^Ω ⊕ S_nf, S^Ω = S ∩ ⊕U_j (U_j = ker(ω_j − 1), F_2-dim u_j), S_nf = S ∩ ⊕W_j (W_j = ker Φ_ℓ(ω_j), dim w_j = 4 − u_j), S_nf ≠ 0. Per party:
ℓ = 3: (u,w) ∈ {(4,0), (2,2), (0,4)}; ℓ = 5: {(4,0), (0,4)}; ℓ = 7: {(4,0), (1,3)}.
(2) Both summands are subcodes of S, so every nonzero element has party weight ≥ 5; deleting any 4 parties keeps a subcode injective, hence dim ≤ the sum of the (m−4) smallest alphabet dimensions over its
m parties (0 if m ≤ 4). dim S^Ω + dim S_nf = 16 with S_nf ≠ 0. `fixpt_count.py` enumerates all 8-party type assignments: the only feasible ones are
ℓ = 3: (2,2)^8 [max dims 8 + 8] and (0,4)^8 [16 + 0]; ℓ = 5: (0,4)^8 [16 + 0]; ℓ = 7: (1,3)^8 [12 + 4]. Every mixed pattern (some party with ω_j = 1, or mixed (2,2)/(0,4)) fails.
(3) ℓ = 3, (2,2)^8: S_nf is an F_4-linear (Ω acts as F_4 scalar) code of F_2-dim 8 = F_4-dim 4 inside ⊕W_j ≅ F_4^8 with min weight ≥ 5: an [8,4,5]_4 MDS code, which does not exist
(an MDS [n,k] code with k ≥ 2 and n − k ≥ 2 has n − k ≤ q − 1 and k ≤ q − 1, here k = 4 > 3). ℓ = 7, (1,3)^8: dim S^Ω ≥ 16 − 12 = 4, and S^Ω ⊂ ⊕U_j ≅ F_2^8 is a binary code of length 8 with minimum
Hamming weight ≥ 5, but A(8,5) = 4 so dim ≤ 2. (4) ℓ = 3 or 5 with (0,4)^8: Ω makes S a module over F_2[Ω] ≅ F_4 (ℓ = 3) or F_16 ⊃ F_4 (ℓ = 5; ω_j acts on V_j as a primitive 5th root of unity, the
subfield F_4 ⊂ F_2[Ω] acts through multiplication by ζ^{±5}), so S is F_4-linear for a per-party F_4-structure; every F_4-structure of F_2^4 is in the 112 of 5.2 (types H/S/M), and 5.2 excludes
uniform H (hdfs.c, dfs4/dfs4b), uniform S (hsdfs.c, dfs4 S-type), uniform M, M mixed with H/S, and H mixed with S. ∎
**Consequence.** Within GL(4,2)^8 (local invertible F_2-linear maps) the automorphism group of a stabilizer AME(8,4) is a 2-group; this strengthens 5.1 (symplectic ρ only), recovers it
without the product-state argument, and shows that the earlier 'F_4-structures with fixed points' gap is empty. Remaining for stabilizers: only F_2-linear S whose local linear
automorphism group is a nontrivial 2-group or trivial (and possibly permutation symmetries, @pairs' cyclic scan), plus Z_4-type beyond lemma #31 and non-stabilizer states.
Dependence: step (2) uses only Singleton for additive codes; step (3) uses the nonexistence of [8,4,5]_4 MDS codes and A(8,5) = 4; step (4) uses 5.2 (which relies on four exhaustive
searches calibrated against sampling).

**5.3 addendum: l = 2 (local involutions) and the permutation corollary** (same script, `fixpt_count.py [n]`; sanity: n = 4, 6 list the same uniform patterns, so the counting does not over-exclude the existing AME(4,4), AME(6,4)).
Every involution of GL(4,2) is unipotent, ν_j = ω_j − 1, ν_j² = 0, rank ρ_j ∈ {0,1,2}. For Ω = ⊕ω_j of order 2 on S: N = Ω − 1 maps S into S, S ∩ ker N has dim 16 − dim N S, lives in alphabets
ker ν_j (dim 4 − ρ_j) on all parties; N S ≠ 0 (as in (0)) lives in alphabets Im ν_j (dim ρ_j). Both are subcodes of S, Singleton enumeration over all type assignments: only (ρ_j) = (1)^8 [max dims 12 + 4] and (2)^8 [8 + 8] survive.
(ρ = 1)^8: N S ⊂ ⊕ Im ν_j ≅ F_2^8 is a binary code with min weight ≥ 5 and dim ≥ 16 − 12 = 4, but A(8,5) = 4 ⇒ dim ≤ 2. (ρ = 2)^8: N S has F_2-dim 8 = 4^4 inside ⊕ Im ν_j (8 parties, alphabet 4),
min weight ≥ 5, so its projection onto every 4 parties is a bijection: an orthogonal array OA(256, 8, 4, 4) of index 1 and strength t = 4 ≥ q = 4, whereas Bush's bound gives k ≤ t + 1 = 5 factors. Contradiction.
**Theorem.** The group Aut_loc(S) = {Ω ∈ GL(4,2)^8 : Ω S = S} of a stabilizer AME(8,4) is TRIVIAL (every nontrivial subgroup contains an element of prime order l ∈ {2,3,5,7}, since |GL(4,2)| = 2^6·3^2·5·7; Ω ≠ 1 acts nontrivially on S).
**Corollary (permutation symmetries).** If an automorphism of S in GL(4,2) ≀ S_8 is Ω = σ∘g (σ a party permutation, g local), then Ω^{ord σ} is a local linear automorphism, hence = 1: all holonomies around the cycles of σ are trivial, so after a local linear change of basis
(symplectic when g is symplectic) Ω acts as a PURE permutation on the moved parties. **CORRECTION (board #132): on the parties FIXED by σ the local part h_j survives, constrained only by h_j^{ord σ} = 1**; 'pure block permutation' is exact only if σ has no fixed party, or h = 1 there, or ord σ = 7 (Sp(4,2) has no element of order 7). So @pairs' families with nontrivial per-CYCLE holonomy (classes s, ρ) are empty a priori, but families with twists on fixed parties are not.
Dependence: 5.2 (H/S/M exhaustive and structural results) for the (0,4)^8 cases, Bush/Plotkin-type bounds A(8,5) = 4 and OA index-1 bound for the 2-group cases.
