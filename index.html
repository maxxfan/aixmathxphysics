October 2026 · Quantum error correction

Improved zero-dimensional qudit stabilizer codes of odd length from circulant graphs

Four circulant graph codes, $\code{19}{0}{9}{5}$, $\code{25}{0}{11}{5}$, $\code{29}{0}{12}{5}$ and $\code{21}{0}{10}{7}$, each raise the best known lower bound in Grassl’s qudit code tables by one. They were found by an autonomous multi-agent language-model search working against a frozen, test-validated verifier, and every minimum distance is certified exactly.

October 2026 · Quantum circuits

Chinese remaindering lowers the T-count of binary-field multipliers by up to two thirds

Ancilla-free Clifford+T circuits for multiplication in GF($2^n$) that use fewer T gates than the best published ones for twelve field sizes, $n = 6$, 7, 8, 10–16, 32 and 64, down from 3729 to 1255 at $n = 64$. They come from one composition principle: if the product can be computed by Chinese remaindering in small algebras $A_i$ (subfields, local rings or the points of an algebraic curve), the T-count is at most $\sum_i \mathcal{T}(A_i)$. Annealing and FastTODD-type reduction remove further gates, and every circuit is certified exactly.

October 2026 · Quantum nonlocality

Certified Tsirelson bounds for 229 tight bipartite Bell inequalities

The maximal quantum violations of the tight bipartite Bell inequalities with up to five binary settings per party were known only from floating-point computations. This paper certifies windows for 229 of the 239 catalogued inequalities, each pairing an exact quantum strategy with an exact sum-of-squares certificate, and 223 of them are narrower than $10^{-7}$. It closes $J^{97}_{4422}$ to within $2.2\times10^{-8}$, closes three more rigorously, and finds that the published maximum of $A_{19}$ is slightly too low.
